3. Customer workflow provider categories

Depending on the Order and customer instructions, Shifter services may use provider categories such as Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, CRM/helpdesk/spreadsheet/database/knowledge-base systems, Slack, Airtable, Notion, Zapier, Make, n8n, email providers, messaging platforms, cloud hosting, databases, logging, monitoring, queueing, storage, billing, contracts, e-signature, support and analytics tools.

4. Customer-directed systems

Many connected tools are chosen, owned or authorised by the Customer. Customer-directed systems are governed by the Customer's own contracts, permissions and account settings. Shifter is not responsible for third-party terms, availability, policy changes or data handling outside the services we control.

5. AI providers and model services

Shifter may use AI providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google AI, Microsoft Azure AI / Azure OpenAI, xAI or comparable providers depending on the workflow, customer needs and available configuration. We aim to use provider settings and workflows appropriate for business data and customer instructions.

We do not intentionally use Customer Personal Data to train third-party public AI models unless expressly agreed or provider settings/terms support no-training for that data.

6. Updates and objections

We may update this page when we add, remove or materially change subprocessors or provider categories. Customers may object to a new subprocessor on reasonable data protection grounds by contacting legal@agentsdub.ai. We will work in good faith to address reasonable objections, which may include using an alternative provider, disabling a feature or allowing termination of the affected service according to the applicable Order.
