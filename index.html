What is Answer Engine Optimization?

Helping buyers find your expertise in AI-generated answers. AEO makes content clear, direct, and supported by evidence that answer engines can understand and use.

SEO gets your pages found and crawled. AEO makes your company the direct answer. GEO improves whether generative AI cites or recommends you. Shifter works across the three as one visibility system.
