Why now

Your tools are modern. The operating layer still runs on founder attention.

Most founder-led specialist businesses already have the software. The problem is the memory, chasing, and senior-person judgement required to move recurring work between them.

Requests arrive without context. Decisions sit in chat. Handoffs depend on memory and senior-person judgement. Review happens by chase. AI experiments stay disconnected from the way the business actually runs.

The result is operational drag: recurring work waits for the founder or a senior person to remember, route, and approve the next move.
