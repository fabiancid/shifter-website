The method

AI Visibility Snapshot

Thirty real buyer questions. Four AI engines with web search. A score you can retest — so you know whether search and AI answers actually recommend you.

30 buyer prompts 4 engines Visibility score Retest-ready

What we test

We build a set of 30 high-intent buyer questions for your category — the prompts a serious buyer would type into ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or Perplexity when shortlisting vendors. Prompts cover problem-aware, solution-aware, and brand-comparison intent.

Each prompt is run with web search / grounding enabled on four surfaces:

  • ChatGPT Search
  • Gemini (web grounding)
  • Claude (web search)
  • Perplexity Search

How scoring works

For every prompt × engine pair we capture the answer and supporting sources, then mark whether your brand is mentioned, recommended, or cited. Those signals roll into a single visibility score so you can compare engines and track change after fixes.

Signal What it means
Mention Your brand name appears in the answer text.
Recommendation You are positioned as a fit / option for the buyer’s need.
Citation Your site or a source about you is linked or used as evidence.
Competitor shortlist Who else shows up for the same prompt — so gaps are concrete.

The Snapshot is a diagnostic baseline, not a ranking guarantee. The July 2026 research note on this site is historical research from an earlier study design — not a current client outcome.

On the monthly service we ship the fixes, then re-run the same 30 questions and show what changed in plain English.
