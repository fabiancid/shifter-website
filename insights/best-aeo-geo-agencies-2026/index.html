How to evaluate any AEO / GEO agency

1. "Show me your citation tracking."

Real AEO teams maintain fixed prompt sets — the actual questions buyers ask — and re-run them across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and AI Overviews on a schedule, reporting mention share over time. If the answer to this question is a keyword rank-tracking screenshot, you're looking at SEO in a new coat.

2. "What specifically moved mentions for a client?"

In 2026, the answer involves third-party presence: review platforms, comparison pages, communities, and publications that the engines retrieve and cite. If everything the agency proposes lives on your own domain, that's an SEO scope with AEO branding.

3. "What don't you know yet?"

This discipline is barely two years old. Attribution is messy, the engines change retrieval behavior constantly, and results show up as directional mention share before they show up as clean pipeline numbers. The credible agencies will tell you that unprompted. Anyone guaranteeing AI visibility is selling harder than the data supports.

Red flags, whoever you talk to

"Guaranteed AI visibility." Nobody controls what a model says. Directional mention share is the honest unit of progress.

A deck full of impressive GEO case studies. The measurement layer is too young. Ask for methodology: prompt sets, re-run cadence, how they separate their impact from noise.

Everything scoped on your own domain. On-site content matters, but AI engines lean heavily on third-party sources. A proposal with no off-site component isn't an AEO proposal.

"Independently reviewed" listicles that rank the author's agency near the top. This industry is drowning in them. Check the byline against the winners. (Yes, that's also why this article opens with a disclosure.)

FAQ

What's the difference between AEO, GEO, and LLM SEO?

They overlap heavily, and many practitioners use the labels interchangeably. A useful working distinction is that AEO focuses on making content easy to surface as a direct answer, while GEO focuses on visibility inside a synthesized AI response. Google treats both as part of good SEO for its own search experiences. See our full SEO vs AEO vs GEO explainer.

Can't my current SEO agency just do this?

Maybe — ask them the three questions above. The disciplines overlap, but AI engines cite a substantially different set of sources than Google's top results, and the measurement stack (prompt tracking, mention share) doesn't exist in a traditional SEO dashboard.

How fast does AEO work show results?

Third-party mentions can appear in answer engines within weeks because retrieval-based engines like Perplexity pull live sources. Becoming a consistent default recommendation in your category is a multi-month project, and anyone quoting days is selling.

How do I check my own AI visibility right now, for free?

Ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Mode the five questions your buyers ask before purchasing, and note who gets named. That's most of a baseline audit. If you're not in the answers and your competitors are, you have a visibility problem worth pricing out.
